Guides for the Curious Barista

French Press Mastery

Steep 30 g of Ember Roast in 500 ml of 93 °C water for 4 minutes. Press slowly, serve, and taste the smoky depth.

Read Guide

Pour‑Over Precision

Use 20 g of Oak Roast, bloom 45 ml for 30 s, then pour 250 ml in a circular motion. Result: bright caramel notes with a clean finish.

Read Guide

Aeropress Espresso‑Style

Invert 15 g of River Roast, 60 ml water, 30 s brew, 30 s pressure. A bright, espresso‑like shot without the machine.
