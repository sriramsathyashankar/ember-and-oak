About Ember & Oak

Our Story

Founded in 2018 in the heart of Portland, Oregon, Ember & Oak began as a small‑batch experiment between two lifelong friends—Maya, a former barista turned cupping specialist, and Jonah, a mechanical engineer with a passion for heat transfer. What started as a weekend hobby quickly grew into a dedicated roastery after they discovered that the same precision they applied to engines could be harnessed to coax the hidden flavors of coffee beans.

Today, we roast in a 1,200‑lb drum that we built in‑house, allowing us to control every variable—from bean intake temperature to cooling airflow—with the same care we’d give a fine piece of machinery. Our name reflects that balance: the ember represents the controlled heat of the roast, while the oak symbolizes the sturdy, sustainable roots of the farms we partner with.

Our Mission

We exist to create coffee that tells a story in every sip. By sourcing directly from smallholder farms that practice shade‑grown, organic, and fair‑trade methods, we ensure that each cup supports both the environment and the people who cultivate the beans. Our mission is three‑fold:

  • Deliver consistently exceptional flavor profiles through meticulous roasting.
  • Foster transparent, long‑term relationships with growers.
  • Educate our community about the journey from farm to cup.

Roasting Philosophy

Every bean has a unique “sweet spot” where its intrinsic sweetness, acidity, and body align. Our process begins with a detailed cupping session where we map those peaks. Using a custom‑built drum and a programmable temperature curve, we dial in the exact heat profile that brings out the bean’s signature notes—whether that’s the bright citrus of an Ethiopian Yirgacheffe or the chocolate‑nut depth of a Colombian Supremo.

We roast in small batches, allowing us to adjust on the fly and maintain consistency across shipments. The result is a lineup of coffees that are vibrant, balanced, and unmistakably Ember & Oak.

Meet the Team

Maya Patel – Head of Quality & Cupping. With a decade of barista experience and a certified Q Grader credential, Maya leads our sensory evaluation and training programs.

Jonah Reed – Master Roaster & Engineer. Jonah designs our roasting equipment and fine‑tunes each profile to perfection.

Lena Kim – Sustainability & Partnerships. Lena travels to farms twice a year, ensuring our sourcing remains ethical and environmentally sound.

Sustainability

We offset 100 % of our operational carbon footprint through verified reforestation projects in Central America. All of our packaging is compostable or recyclable, and we encourage customers to bring their own reusable containers for bulk purchases.
